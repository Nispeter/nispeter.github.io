## Overview

Back-end engineer on *Krunchbox-reimagined*, a **multitenant SaaS** platform for
**retail analytics and business intelligence**. Retailers feed in their data and tenants get
real-time tracking of **stock, units and margins**, live visualization, stock predictions and
allocation, and store, product, promo and warehouse management — surfaced through reports,
dashboards, AI insights and alerts.

My work is the **API and database layer**: performant data access and services over large
retail datasets.

## Features Developed

- **Limited Access Users** — standalone feature: scoped access for restricted tenant users.
- **Currency conversion** — standalone feature applied across reports and dashboards.
- **Store master & product master** — implementation of the master-data modules.
- **Reports, dashboards & promo master** — debugging and fixes across existing modules.

## Tech Stack

- **Language:** Python
- **Framework:** FastAPI, Uvicorn
- **Databases:** ClickHouse, SQL Server, Redis
- **Auth:** Auth0, JWT
- **Testing & quality:** Playwright, flake8, mypy
- **Tooling:** Claude Code, MCP, Bitbucket, Jira
